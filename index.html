About Me

Hello,
I'm Dhruv.

I've always been fascinated by how businesses work—why some decisions create impact while others don't. That curiosity led me to Business Analytics, where I discovered that data isn't just about understanding the past; it's about solving real business problems, building better products, and creating measurable value.

Today, I enjoy combining data, analytics, and emerging AI tools to build intelligent solutions—from dashboards and automation workflows to data-driven products. Along the way, I've developed strong foundations in SQL, Python, data modelling, dashboard development, and product thinking, always focusing on the business problem before the technical solution.

Whether I'm analysing data, building an analytics pipeline, or exploring AI-powered workflows, my goal remains the same: to turn complex business challenges into simple, scalable, and impactful solutions.
